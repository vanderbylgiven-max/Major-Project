Who We Are

A University Built for the Bold

Founded in 1991 and headquartered in Cyberjaya, Malaysia, Limkokwing University of Creative Technology is a pioneering private institution with campuses across Asia, Africa and Europe. Our Namibia campus brings that global vision to the heart of Southern Africa.

We believe in education that doesn't just teach — it transforms. Through hands-on learning, industry partnerships and a culture of creativity, our graduates leave ready to lead.
