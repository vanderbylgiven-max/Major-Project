Academic Programs & Admission Requirements

1. List of Programs Offered

  • Computing: Software Development, Cyber Security
  • Education: Geography and English Specializations
  • Commerce: Accounting, Business Administration
  • Natural Sciences: Environmental Science, Biology

2. Course Descriptions and Duration

Program Type Description Duration
Certificate Introductory skills for entry-level roles. 1 Year
Diploma Technical and vocational training. 2 - 3 Years
Bachelor's Degree Deep theoretical and practical knowledge. 3 - 4 Years

3. Admission Requirements

General entry for 2026 academic year:

  • Degree: 25+ points, Minimum 'C' in English (NSSCO).
  • Diploma: 22+ points, Minimum 'D' in English (NSSCO).
  • Mature Age: For applicants 25 years or older with experience.
