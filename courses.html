2026 Entry Criteria

Admission Requirements

All applicants must have completed the Namibia Senior Secondary Certificate Ordinary level (NSSCO) or equivalent. Mature age entry is available for applicants 25 years or older.

Bachelor's Degree

25 pts minimum
  • Minimum grade C in English (NSSCO)
  • Relevant subject credits per faculty
  • Certified NSSCO results required

Diploma

22 pts minimum
  • Minimum grade D in English (NSSCO)
  • Two relevant subject passes
  • Certified NSSCO results required

Certificate

18 pts minimum
  • Minimum grade E in English (NSSCO)
  • Open to school leavers and working adults
  • Interview may be required

Mature Age Entry

25 yrs+
  • Relevant work experience considered
  • Portfolio or aptitude test may apply
  • English proficiency required
